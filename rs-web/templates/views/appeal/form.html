If you think your account was banned or muted by mistake, you can ask us to look at it again. A member of staff who didn't make the original decision will review your appeal.

  • Log in with your RuneScape username and password.
  • Explain clearly why you think the decision was wrong. Be honest: appeals with false information will be upheld.
  • You can appeal each ban or mute once. You will be able to see the result on the appeal status page.
