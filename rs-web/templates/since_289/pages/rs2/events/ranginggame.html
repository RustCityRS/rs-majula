Ranging Mini Game

The Ranging Guild is the home of a simple mini game. Players score points by shooting at targets. The reward tickets you get from this game may be exchanged for useful ranging items at one of the Guild's shops, including dragonhide armour, rune arrows and barbed tips for your crossbow bolts. Pay the Competition Judge 200 coins to start.
