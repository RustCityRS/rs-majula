Games Room

The Prince of Burthorpe is well known for his love of games and puzzles. The Prince has decided to open his games rooms to the public, this way he can attract the best gamers from around RuneScape!

The stairs down to the Burthorpe Games Rooms can be found in the entrance hall of the Prince's Castle in Burthorpe. There is no entrance fee and there is a bar to relax in. Currently you can play Runelink and Draughts. Draughts uses standard rules apart from: a draw is declared if no piece has been taken or promoted for forty moves. In Runelink you have to connect four runes in a row to win.

You can challenge another player by right clicking them and selecting "Challenge", you then choose the game and can modify some options. You can choose the rune that you want to use as your game piece at this point. The other options are: the time per move allowed and whether the game will affect your rank or not.

You start off at rank 1000, when you play someone else you automatically "stake" some of your rank and the other player does the same. When playing someone of much lower level you will stake a lot but the other player will stake very little. On the other hand if you are playing someone much higher than you then you will stake very little but stand to gain a lot. If you win then you will gain the other players stake, if you lose you will lose what you staked.

Whilst you can challenge players anywhere in the games rooms there are two dedicated challenge rooms. If you go into one of the rooms you will see the board for the game appear on your main window. You will also be able to see your own rank and other players ranks when you right click them. If you become experienced enough you will be able to go up the ladder to the experienced challenge room where you only find other experienced players.

The empty rooms will be used for future expansion. If the games rooms prove to be popular then these rooms will be opened up for other board games such as Slime Wars and Chess.

Time to brush up on your board games skills!
