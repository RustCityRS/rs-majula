The Fishing Trawler

Murphy's boat moored at Port Khazard has a few holes in it but he's not worried, he's an experienced sailor after all! Murphy says he doesn't need help but if your offering he could use the extra pair of hands. There is lots to do on board the boat so it's a good idea to take on a few friends to help out.

You'll need at least level 15 fishing to help catch any fish; Murphy will give you a share of the catch if the boat gets back safe and sound. The net has a tendency to break, you'll need some rope to repair it, the crafting skill will help here but it's not a requirement.

You'll need swamp paste to fill up any leaks, the shops in Port Khazard sell some. If you want to make it for yourself the recipe for swamp paste is: swamp tar mixed with flour and then cooked. Swamp tar can be found in the swamps south of Lumbridge.

The Port Khazard shops also sell bailing buckets, if the boat starts to fill with water you will be glad you have one!
