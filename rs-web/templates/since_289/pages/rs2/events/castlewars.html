Castle Wars

The Castle Wars Arena has opened south of Ardougne. Saradomin and Zamorak have taken an interest and are urging their chosen teams towards victory. Who will you choose to fight for? Or will you follow the way of Guthix and help keep the balance?

Castle Wars is a combat based team game with a single game lasting 20 minutes.
The Zamorakians and the Saradominists try to infiltrate each others castles, capture each others standards and take them back through enemy lines to their own base and claim a victory point for their chosen deity.

Speak to Lanthus in the Castle Wars waiting area for more information on how to play. Buy a manual off him for more information about the features and tactics you can use whilst playing.

There are no requirements to join in (apart from being a RuneScape Member), just turn up and choose a side!
