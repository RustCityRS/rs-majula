The Watch Tower Quest

Main Menu - Manual - Quest Index

The Wizards of the Yanille Watch Tower have been reporting increased ogre sightings recently. Use a wide variety of your skills to help the wizards recover their stolen power crystals and stop the advance of the ogre hordes!

Start point:  Watch Tower close to city gates of Yanille
To start:  Speak to Watch Tower Wizard
Quest length:  Long
Minimum requirements:  Level 14 Herblore
Level 14 Magic
Level 15 Thieving
Level 25 Agility
Level 40 Mining
Must defeat Level 58 monsters
Members only:  Yes
