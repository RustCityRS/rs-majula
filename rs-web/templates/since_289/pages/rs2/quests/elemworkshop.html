The Elemental Workshop

Main Menu - Manual - Quest Index

Hundreds of years ago a mineral was found that had the ability to change the property of magic.

The magicians, fearing the effect this may have on there profession, sealed this workshop forever; or so they had hoped. See if you can rediscover the lost knowledge of elemental ore.
