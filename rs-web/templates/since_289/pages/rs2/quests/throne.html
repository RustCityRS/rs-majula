Throne of Miscellania

Main Menu - Manual - Quest Index

Rumours have been coming back from Miscellania that all is not well, it is said that the king has been turned into a monster! An exceptional hero is required to investigate. Experience in ruling kingdoms, wooing royality and negotiating peace would be an advantage. Apply within.

Start point:  Miscellania, take the ship from Rellekka
To start:  Speak to King Vargas
Quest length:  Medium
Minimum requirements:  Hero's Quest
The Fremennik Trials
It would be an advantage to have at least one of the following skills:
Level 35 Herblore
Level 45 Woodcutting
Members only:  Yes
